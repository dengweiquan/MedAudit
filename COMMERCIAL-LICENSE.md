to provide permission-audit services to third parties — must hold a
commercial license. Personal use and non-production use (evaluation,
testing, development, laboratory) are free under the license shipped
with the software (the Weixun Source License 1.0) and need no
commercial license. See `LICENSING.md`.

### What the subscription includes

- **Per-site annual subscription** (recommended billing unit, draft):
  one "site" is one hospital deployment of MedAudit (the platform is
  deployed for a single hospital per installation).
- The right to use the licensed versions in production at the
  licensed site(s) for the subscription term.
- All updates released during the subscription term.
- Email support during the subscription term (response-time targets:
  TBD).
