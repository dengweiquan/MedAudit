## English

### Who needs it

Any for-profit organization that uses MedAudit in a **production
environment** — including running the MedAudit platform for its own
business operations, or using it to provide audit services to third
parties — must hold a commercial license. Personal use and
non-production use (evaluation, testing, development, laboratory) are
free under the Business Source License 1.1 and need no commercial
license. See `LICENSING.md`.

### What the subscription includes

- The right to use the licensed versions in production, within the
  licensed scope (sites and audit targets) stated in the agreement, for
  the subscription term.
- All updates released during the subscription term.
- Email support during the subscription term (response-time targets are
  stated in the signed agreement).

### Pricing and payment

- Fees, the billing unit, and the licensed scope are agreed in the
  signed agreement.
- Licensor, contracting and invoicing entity: 厦门维讯信息科技有限公
  司 (Xiamen Weixun Information Technology Co., Ltd.). Taxes are as
  stated in the signed agreement.
- Contact for a quotation: **sales@xmweixun.com**

### Restrictions

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the licensee may
not:

- resell, rent, or sublicense the software or the license;
- use the software to provide hosted or managed services (including
  audit services) to third parties — service-provider use requires
  separate service-provider terms;
- exceed the licensed scope of production use.
