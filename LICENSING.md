## English

### The license

MedAudit is licensed under the **Business Source License 1.1
(BSL 1.1)**, as set out in the `LICENSE` file.

### What BSL 1.1 means

- The source code remains publicly available for anyone to read, audit,
  copy, modify, and redistribute under the BSL terms ("source
  available").
- **Non-production use is free** for everyone, including commercial
  organizations: evaluation, testing, development, and laboratory use.
- **Personal use is free.**
- **Production use by a for-profit organization requires a commercial
  license** obtained from the Licensor in advance. This includes running
  the MedAudit platform for the organization's own business operations,
  and using MedAudit to provide audit services to third parties.
- Every version converts automatically to the **MIT License** four
  years after that version is first made available, at which point it
  becomes fully open source under MIT terms.

### Production vs. non-production: examples

- A hospital's IT department installs MedAudit on test systems to
  evaluate it before procurement. → **Non-production evaluation**;
  free.
- An engineer studies or tests MedAudit in a personal home lab. →
  **Non-production, personal use**; free.
- A for-profit organization runs the MedAudit platform in production to
  audit the permissions of its own production systems on an ongoing
  basis. → **Production use**; a commercial license is required.
- An IT service provider uses MedAudit to deliver permission-audit
  services to its customers (for example, hospitals). → **Production
  use**; a commercial license is required, and providing services to
  third parties may require specific service-provider terms (see
  `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`).

### An honest note on "open source"

The Business Source License is **not** an Open Source license as
defined by the Open Source Initiative, because it restricts production
use. We describe MedAudit as **source available**, not open source. The
time limit is real, though: each version becomes MIT-licensed open
source four years after its release.

## 中文

### 许可证

MedAudit 采用 **Business Source License 1.1（BSL 1.1）**，正文见
随附的 `LICENSE` 文件。

### BSL 1.1 意味着什么

- 源码继续公开，任何人都可以按 BSL 条款阅读、审计、复制、修改和再
  分发（即「源码可见 / source available」）。
- **非生产用途对所有人免费**，包括商业组织：评估、测试、开发、实验
  室环境使用。
- **个人使用免费。**
- **营利性组织在生产环境中使用，必须事先取得 Licensor（许可方）的
  商业授权。**这包括为组织自身业务运行 MedAudit 平台，以及利用
  MedAudit 为第三方提供审计服务。
- 每个版本在其发布满 4 年后自动转为 **MIT 许可证**，届时成为完全按
  MIT 条款的开源软件。

### 生产与非生产：具体例子

- 某医院信息科在测试系统上安装 MedAudit 进行采购前评估。→ **非生产
  评估**，免费。
- 某工程师在个人家庭实验室学习、测试 MedAudit。→ **非生产的个人使
  用**，免费。
- 某营利性组织在生产环境中长期运行 MedAudit 平台，审计其自身生产系
  统的权限。→ **生产使用**，需要商业授权。
- 某 IT 服务商使用 MedAudit 为其客户（例如医院）提供权限审计服
  务。→ **生产使用**，需要商业授权；为第三方提供服务还可能需要单独
  的服务商条款（见 `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`）。

### 关于「开源」的诚实说明

Business Source License **不是** OSI（Open Source Initiative）定义
的开源许可证，因为它限制生产用途。我们将 MedAudit 称为**源码可见
（source available）**，不称开源。但时间限制是真实的：每个版本在发
布 4 年后转为 MIT 开源许可。
