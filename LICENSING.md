### What the Weixun Source License means

- The source code remains publicly available for anyone to read,
  audit, copy, modify, and redistribute under the license terms
  ("source available").
- **Non-production use is free** for everyone, including commercial
  organizations: evaluation, testing, development, and laboratory
  use.
- **Personal use is free.**
- **Production use by a for-profit organization requires a commercial
  license** obtained from the Licensor in advance. This includes
  running the MedAudit platform for the organization's own business
  operations, and using MedAudit to provide audit services to third
  parties.
- **The license does not convert to an open source license**, and no
  additional rights arise with the passage of time.

### Production vs. non-production: examples

- A hospital's IT department installs MedAudit on test systems to
  evaluate it before procurement. → **Non-production evaluation**;
  free.
- An engineer studies or tests MedAudit in a personal home lab. →
  **Non-production, personal use**; free.
- A for-profit organization runs the MedAudit platform in production
  to audit the permissions of its own production systems on an
  ongoing basis. → **Production use**; a commercial license is
  required.
- An IT service provider uses MedAudit to deliver permission-audit
  services to its customers (for example, hospitals). →
  **Production use**; a commercial license is required, and providing
  services to third parties may require specific service-provider
  terms (see `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`).

### An honest note on "open source"

The Weixun Source License is **not** an Open Source license as
defined by the Open Source Initiative, because it restricts
production use. We describe MedAudit as **source available**, not
open source, and this license sets no date on which the work becomes
open source.

- 源码继续公开，任何人都可以按许可证条款阅读、审计、复制、修

  权利。

### 生产与非生产：具体例子

- 某医院信息科在测试系统上安装 MedAudit 进行采购前评估。→
  **非生产评估**，免费。

### 关于「开源」的诚实说明

维讯源码许可证**不是** OSI（Open Source Initiative）定义的开
源许可证，因为它限制生产用途。我们将 MedAudit 称为**源码可见
（source available）**，不称开源；本许可证不设定软件转为开源
的日期。
